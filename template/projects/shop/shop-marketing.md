# Shop marketing (example)

## What we sell
Specialty coffee in 12 oz bags (USD 18) and a monthly two-bag subscription (USD 32). Source: [[projects/shop/decisions/2026-01-10-launch-price|price decision]].

## For whom
People aged 25 to 45 who brew at home and want something better than supermarket coffee. To confirm with interviews.

## The promise
"Freshly roasted coffee at your door every month." **We cannot promise:** health benefits, "the best coffee in the world", 24-hour shipping.

## Product truths
- Roasted the week it ships (source: the shop's process, to confirm in writing).
- Free shipping from two bags (source: the shop's shipping page).

## Voice
Warm and unpretentious. Yes: "tasty", "fresh", "your morning coffee". No: "premium", "exclusive", tasting jargon.

- [[knowledge/channels/instagram|Instagram]]: active account, 3 reels a week.
