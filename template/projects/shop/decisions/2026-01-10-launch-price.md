# Launch price: USD 18 a bag and USD 32 for the subscription

**Context.** We needed a clear price to start selling.

**Decision.** A 12 oz bag at USD 18. A two-bag monthly subscription at USD 32. Free shipping from two bags.

**Discarded alternatives.** USD 15 a bag (no margin left after shipping); subscription only (scares first-time buyers).

**What changes.** [[projects/shop/shop-marketing|Marketing]] and [[projects/shop/shop-status|status]].
